About Rodd

A plaintiff's advocate first. A strategist for the rest of his career.

Rodd became an equity partner the day he was sworn in as an attorney, and spent the next three decades trying cases for plaintiffs in personal injury law, arguing before courts from county level up to the United States Supreme Court.

He went on to serve as CEO of HALT, a 20,000-member national legal advocacy organization based in Washington, DC, leading pro-consumer legal reform that drew national coverage in the Wall Street Journal, the Des Moines Register, and other major outlets.

Today, as Vice President of Strategic Partnerships at Synergy, he brings that trial and advocacy background to legal consulting and lien resolution, work most consultants approach only from the outside looking in.
